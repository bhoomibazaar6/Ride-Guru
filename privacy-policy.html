🔒 Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

1. 📋 Data Collection

We only collect data that is strictly necessary for the app to function — your name, phone number, and search history. No sensitive personal data is stored without your explicit consent.

2. 🔐 Data Security

Your data is kept secure using SSL encryption. We never sell your data to any third party. Only anonymised data is shared with ride platforms solely for fare comparison purposes.

3. 📍 Location Data

Your location is used only at the time of ride comparison. We do not track your location in the background. You can turn off location permissions at any time from your device settings.

4. 🍪 Cookies & Analytics

We use basic analytics to improve the app experience. This data is fully anonymised and cannot be used to identify any individual user.

5. 🗑️ Data Deletion

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time. Upon request, all your data will be permanently deleted within 30 days.

6. 📞 Contact for Privacy

For any privacy-related queries, please contact us at: rideguru7@gmail.com
